# Cooooooooooins!

Honestly, have you ever seen a level with <cy>10 coins</c>?

Well, guess what! These aren't edited videos anymore <cg>they're real!!</c>

The current version contains only <cy>10</c> fully playable main levels with <cy>10 coins</c>. We'll be adding more in the future!

It's so absurd, funny, and entertaining to watch that even when you're playing it, it looks like an edited video

Have fun with it! But before you install it, there are a few things you should know:

- The mod creates <cf>separate stats</c> from the ones you have in the game, so don't be surprised if your account doesn't show up, just disable the mod and your stats will be restored

- <cr>**YOU CANNOT LOG IN WITH YOUR ACCOUNT!**</c> This is due to the obvious use of a cheat that will be detected if you complete a level with <cy>10 coins</c>

- If, despite the explanations above, you insist on trying to link your account to the mod using external methods, as the developer of this mod, <cr>I am not responsible for leaderboard bans</c>. So please, one last time <cj>*(please use it responsibly)*</c>
